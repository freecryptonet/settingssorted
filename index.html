Settings Sorted

Short, clear how-to videos for Windows, apps and browsers.

Settings Sorted is a YouTube channel with step-by-step screen recordings: where a setting is, what to click, and why it matters. Videos are recorded on a clean test PC, narrated, and checked before they go online.

Watch on YouTube: @settingssorted

YT How-To Pipeline

"YT How-To Pipeline" is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only for the Settings Sorted channel: to upload its videos, captions and thumbnails, to add them to playlists, and to read the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to other users.
