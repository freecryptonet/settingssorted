Privacy Policy

Settings Sorted · YT How-To Pipeline · last updated 22 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers "YT How-To Pipeline", the publishing tool of the YouTube channel Settings Sorted. The tool is used only by the channel owner, for the owner's own channel. It has no other users and no website sign-up.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's videos, captions and thumbnails, and set their title, description, tags and privacy;
  • create the channel's playlists and add its videos to them;
  • read the channel's own video list and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources, search terms).

It does not access any other channel, any viewer's account, comments' authors' accounts, or any personal data of viewers. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated and contains no personal information.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and reports are stored only on the channel owner's computer, in a folder that only the owner's Windows account can read. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The tool sends data only to Google (YouTube) itself.

Retention and deletion

Reports are kept as local files until the owner deletes them. Access can be withdrawn at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; deleting the local token files removes the stored sign-in.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data held by Google.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
